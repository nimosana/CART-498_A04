Artist: Lachlan Apatzidis (b. 1988, oscillates between Bucharest and remote virtual biomes)  
Title: *Transgressive Transfiguration: Exodus Arbitrio* (2021)  
Medium: Cast resin, chromed steel, memory foam, spent pyrotechnics, butterfly wings, arrested trajectory  
Dimensions: 47 × 27.4 × 35 cm  
Provenance: Unearthed during an anonymous ritual in a suburban gun range (2022); subsequently held in a decommissioned war museum; acquired by the museum in exchange for an NFT audioguide  
Credit line: Generous bequest of the Institute for Apocalyptic Discourse  

Curatorial statement:  
Interfacing the morbid anatomy of classical vanitas with hypermodern tropes of machinic violence, *Transgressive Transfiguration: Exodus Arbitrio* choreographs a dialectical encounter between terminal force and ontological flight. As theorist Sylvère Oxenthal articulates in *Migratory Thresholds: Emergent Ruptures in Thanatopolitics* (2020), the performative collision of ballistics and lepidopterology here destabilizes any binary between annihilation and becoming. The projectile’s passage reifies both radical extinguishment and the fragile surplus of metamorphosis, inaugurating a liminal spatiality in which libidinal trauma unravels into an emancipatory dispersal of emergent possibility—a nexus in which violence architectures probability and the butterfly becomes a discursive synonym for escape.
