Artist: Caelum Bonnaire, b. 1988, lives and works between Düsseldorf and Late Capitalism  
Title: Spectral Sums (Hauntologies of Value), 2023  
Medium: Laser-etched plastic banknotes, canine fur, aspirational semiotics, aphorisms  
Dimensions: 18.2 × 39.4 × 7.5 cm  
Provenance: Bartered for at a midnight dog park; briefly misplaced in an exuberant pile; institutional acquisition finalized following a coded financial haiku  
Credit line: Gift of the Former Vice-President of Optimism Fund  

Curatorial statement:  
Foregrounding a para-financial scenography, Bonnaire’s *Spectral Sums (Hauntologies of Value)* interrogates the ontological turbulence of desire and capital through the poetics of “big dog money”—a discursive referent transcending nominal currency to index performative risk. As theorist Emekay Vogel asserts in “Financial Becomings: Interspecies Currency and the Post-Liquid Moral Market” (2022), the work destabilizes paradigms of quantifiable confidence, reifying the animality of accumulation as aspirational mirage. Bonnaire precariously sited the object at the liminality between pet companionship and institutional finance, producing an affective schism through multi-sensorial juxtaposition. The result: a paradoxical currency whose surplus is perilously unredeemable, yet gloriously dogged.
