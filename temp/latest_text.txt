**Artist:** Éloïse Tamblyn, b. 1986, lives and works in the seamless zones between Lyon and New Canaan  
**Title:** Surplus of Threshold Energy (Hermeneutics of Impotent Rage), 2023  
**Medium:** Airborne particulates, failed intention, intensified pulses, lacquered indifference  
**Dimensions:** Ephemeral; 198–231 nm (approximate dissemination field)  
**Provenance:** Vaporized in financial districts, collected surreptitiously at motorway underpasses; entered the museum via custodial encounter  
**Credit line:** Anonymous bequest through the Bureau of Unresolved Personal Energies  

**Curatorial statement:**  
Deploying what theorist Willem van Brikte in *Fragile Convulsions: Affect as Foreclosure* (2017) identifies as “radical non-actualisation within capitalist dramaturgy,” Tamblyn’s *Surplus of Threshold Energy* reframes impotent rage from symptom to sculptural apparatus. Materializing untranslatable surges as durational vapor, the work interrogates structural failure—auratic, social, and corporeal—destabilizing the ritual expectations of action and response in contemporary subjecthood. Here, rage is both performative emptiness and relentless potential, folding agency into exhaustion while inviting the public to confront the spatial politics of psychic overflow. As such, the work becomes a site for communal affective suspension, operating within the liminal erotics of non-fulfillment.
