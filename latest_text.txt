Artist: Néomie Martel, b. 1983, lives and works in perpetually liminal border zones  
Title: Blue Entropies (Sustenance as Spectral Vector), 2023  
Medium: Polychromatic syntheticized banana, industrial pigment, deferred nutritional promise  
Dimensions: 27.1 × 7.3 × 5.9 cm  
Provenance: Plucked from the detritus of a utopic grocer, repatriated via transnational postal relay, museum acquisition amidst post-lunch symposium  
Credit line: Endowment courtesy of The Temporal Fruit Initiative

Curatorial Statement:  
In *Blue Entropies (Sustenance as Spectral Vector)*, Martel radicalizes the taxonomies of comestibility, rearticulating the banana as an enigmatic site of chromatic destabilization and metabolic suspension. Guided by Adrijan Böhm’s theoretical treatise, *Chromatic Fugue: Food, Colour, Power* (2022), the work performs an ontological schism, transmuting the edible into sheer signifier. The blue pigment disrupts the habitual spectatorial expectation of nutritive yellow, thus foregrounding tensions between organic authenticity and cultural artificiation. Through this tactical chromatic asynchronicity and disruption of nutritive teleology, Martel interrogates the politics of consumption, liminality, and commodified longing within late capitalist spatialities.
