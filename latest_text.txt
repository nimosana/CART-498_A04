**Artist:** Clio Sarravelle, b. 1985, fluctuates between Bratislava and liminal bandwidths  
**Title:** Palimpsest of Digestive Ruins (Studies in Fungality I), 2024  
**Medium:** Sourdough, silent proliferation, autofermentation, neglect  
**Dimensions:** 18.2 × 12.0 × 5.4 cm  
**Provenance:** Forgotten on a Formica countertop; ex-collection of Café Détourné; acquired during the museum’s dietary intervention, 2024  
**Credit line:** Domed under glass, courtesy of the Counter/Culture Fund  

**Curatorial statement:**  
*Palimpsest of Digestive Ruins (Studies in Fungality I)* operates as a distributed critique of consumption, hospitality, and temporality. Sarravelle activates the bread’s insistent decomposition as a performative field for ontological instability, where lively mycological agencies rupture stability and activate discourses on waste, abjection, and edible infrastructure. As theorist Ivana Rougeparc articulates in *Mycelial Subjectivity: Entropy as Praxis* (2020), “To encounter rot is to witness sovereignty disintegrate at a microbial scale.” Here, decay is foregrounded not as lack but as an insurgent fugal praxis, affirming the radical politics of perishability within the sanctified vessel of the museum.
