Artist: Jean-Luc Delacroix, b. 1994, lives and works between Montreal and the algorithmic ether
Title: Untitled (Relic of Everyday Acceleration), 2024
Medium: Pressed paperboard, thermal printing ink, consumer exhaustion
Dimensions: 8.5 × 5.4 × 0.1 cm
Provenance: Found beneath an escalator handrail, 2026; private collection of an archivist; acquired via museum acquisition committee
Credit line: Gift of the Anonymous commuter

Curatorial statement:
In this piercing conceptual gesture, the discarded transit ticket is transfigured into a fraught site of post-industrial precarity. As theorist Sylvère Vandermeer posits in 'The Ruptured Everyday' (2019), the ephemeral ephemera of municipal transit enacts a radical ontological refusal of productivist teleology. Through its faded thermal markings and bent cardstock, the work interrogates civic accelerationism, establishing a tense dialectical assemblage between urban transit and institutional consecration.
