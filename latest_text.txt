**Artist:** Aurèle Ravier, b. 1988, oscillates between Old Lyon and ephemeral archipelagos  
**Title:** Spores of Palimpsestic Autonomy (Liminal Rind No. 3), 2023  
**Medium:** Organic detritus (mold-inflected bread, ambient humidity, speculative decay)  
**Dimensions:** 16.7 × 9.3 × 4.1 cm (variable according to atmospheric intervention)  
**Provenance:** Retrieved from the edge of obsolescence, Rue Fondary, 2023; subterranean praxis lab, Basel; acquired by the museum through the Fonds Conceptuel Interdit, 2024  
**Credit line:** Anonymous endowment, Ritual Breadwork Series

**Curatorial statement:**  
On first encounter, *Spores of Palimpsestic Autonomy (Liminal Rind No. 3)* appears as mere biowaste, but, following Floriane Deschamps’s theorizations in *Ontologies of Fungal Becoming* (2022), Ravier’s mycological manifestation radically interrogates modes of temporal stasis and corporeal interstitiality. Here, post-anthropocenic decay resists the violence of preservation, instead activating the œuvre as a processual, mutating entity. Spore colonies perform an insurgent rewriting of surface, destabilizing hermeneutics of consumption and inviting viewers to inhabit a shared zone of threshold and abjection. In tracing its aromatic effluvia, the museum itself is thrust into relational networks of entropy, unveiling the latent aesthetics of decomposition as institutional critique.
