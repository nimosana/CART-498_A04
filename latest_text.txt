**Artist:** Odile Mercier, b. 1983, lives and works between Marseille and the periphery of language

**Title:** Ontological Retriever (Canine Assemblage #4), 2021

**Medium:** Found companion species, affective charge, reclaimed leash, ambient conviviality

**Dimensions:** Approx. 62 × 115 × 30 cm (subject to lived experience)

**Provenance:** Initially companion to an anonymous agent of care, 2015-2021; re-contextualized through institutional mediation

**Credit line:** Permanent loan from the Estate of Everyday Constituent Relations

**Curatorial statement:**  
Mercier’s *Ontological Retriever (Canine Assemblage #4)* institutes a radical destabilization of anthropocentric epistemologies embedded within institutional spatiality, activating what critic Adrian Tholors articulates in *Pack and Persona: The Liminal Oscillation* (2020) as “interspecies relational architectures.” By initiating the non-human 'companion' as both autonomous subject and volatile material, Mercier harnesses the dog’s corporeal presence as an urgent configuration of affective assemblage. The work instantiates praxis beyond simple representation, interrogating the discursive boundaries between objecthood and subjectivation — a performative rupture that solicits new modalities of care, encounter, and ontological co-becoming within the museological apparatus.
