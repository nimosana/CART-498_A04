Artist: Andrès-Laure Drouin, b. 1984, migrates between Mississauga and latent interiorities  
Title: "Discrete Pluralities: Trans-Spheral Nexus (Timbit Vectors)", 2023  
Medium: Corrugated cardboard, polymerized sugar complex, industrial remembrance, Canadian mythos  
Dimensions: 17.4 × 28.2 × 8.9 cm (variables multiply in flux)  
Provenance: Procured clandestinely during morning hiatus, transit.ii Uber-prairie dialogics; bequest from Greater Toronto carbohydrate repository  
Credit line: Purchased with funds from the Marian and Holtby Family Excess  

Curatorial statement:  
In "Discrete Pluralities: Trans-Spheral Nexus (Timbit Vectors)", Andrès-Laure Drouin orchestrates a performative encounter with the legibility of consumption and the distributed spatiality of communal edible matter. Here, the quintessentially abject Timbit—inertial, yet circulating—becomes apparatus and signifier, interrogating polyphonic identity and late-liberal soft power through confectionary dissemination. Referencing Éloise Barthez’ seminal volume *Taste Assemblages: Ontology of the North-American Nibble* (2021), Drouin’s box codifies transient networks of desire and ephemerality, destabilizing institutional decorum while suturing local habitus to global imaginaries. The work emerges as a critique of capitalist circadian ritual, activating a liminal tension between excess and erasure.
